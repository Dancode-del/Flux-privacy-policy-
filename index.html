Fasmora ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Fasmora may collect, how that information is used, stored, and shared, and the choices available to you when using the Fasmora browser.

By using Fasmora, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Fasmora is designed to minimize the amount of personal information it collects.

1.1 Browsing Information

Fasmora allows you to visit websites and interact with online content. Depending on how the App is configured and the websites you visit, information such as:

  • Website addresses (URLs)
  • Search queries entered into websites or search engines
  • Browsing history
  • Cookies and website data
  • Login sessions and website preferences

may be processed by the websites you visit or stored locally by the browser.

Fasmora does not intentionally collect or sell your complete browsing history to us or to third parties.

1.2 Bookmarks

If you save websites as bookmarks, the bookmark information may be stored locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Website title
  • Website URL
  • Bookmark folders or categories

Bookmarks are not intentionally transmitted to us unless a future fasmora feature specifically states otherwise.

1.3 Downloads

Fasmora may allow you to download files from websites. Downloaded files are stored on your device according to your device's storage settings and the permissions granted to Fasmora.

Fasmora does not automatically upload your downloaded files to our servers.

1.4 App and Technical Information

Depending on the services and SDKs included in the version of Fasmora you use, certain technical information may be processed, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • General diagnostic information
  • Crash information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Information about interactions with advertisements

This information may be collected by third-party services used by Fasmora rather than directly by Fasmora.

1.5 Fasmora AI and AI Memory

When you choose to use Fasmora AI, the text you submit and, for page-aware features, relevant content from the webpage you are viewing may be sent to Fasmora's AI service and its service providers to generate a response. Fasmora is designed to avoid sending page context from sensitive pages such as sign-in, password, payment, checkout, and similar security-sensitive pages.

If AI Memory is enabled, Fasmora may store selected information used to personalize future AI conversations. You can disable AI Memory and manage or clear saved AI information using controls provided in the App.

1.6 Incognito Browsing

Incognito mode is designed to reduce information retained by Fasmora after a private browsing session. Incognito browsing history and private AI conversations are not intended to be added to the normal browsing profile or persistent AI Memory. Files you explicitly download and information retained by websites, network providers, or third-party services may remain outside the Incognito session.

1.7 Account and Authentication Information

If you use an optional Fasmora account or sign-in feature, authentication providers and Fasmora's supporting services may process information needed to authenticate you and maintain the service. Fasmora does not receive your third-party account password from an OAuth provider.

2. Advertising

Fasmora may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising providers.

Advertising providers may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location
  • Ad interaction information
  • Diagnostic information

This information may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertising.

Third-party advertising providers have their own privacy policies governing their processing of information.

Fasmora does not control how independent advertising providers process information.

3. Third-Party Websites

Fasmora is a web browser, so you can access websites operated by third parties.

Websites you visit through Fasmora may independently collect information about you, including cookies, IP addresses, account information, browsing activity, or other information.

Fasmora is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any website you visit if you have concerns about how that website handles your information.

4. Search Engines

When you perform a search through a search engine using Fasmora, your search query may be sent directly to that search engine.

The search engine may collect and process your search query, IP address, device information, cookies, and other information according to its own privacy policy.

Fasmora does not control the information collected by search engines.

5. Cookies and Website Storage

Websites accessed through Fasmora may use cookies, local storage, session storage, cache, and similar technologies.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Keep you signed in
  • Remember website preferences
  • Maintain shopping carts or sessions
  • Provide website functionality
  • Measure website usage
  • Deliver personalized content or advertising

Fasmora may store website data locally on your device to provide browser functionality.

You can manage or clear browser data using the controls provided within Fasmora, where available.

6. Permissions

Fasmora may request Android permissions necessary for specific features.

For example, storage-related permissions may be required to save downloaded files on devices or Android versions where such permissions are applicable.

Fasmora only requests permissions that are necessary for the functionality provided by the App.

You can manage app permissions through your Android device's settings.

Camera, microphone, and location access may also be requested by websites. Where supported, Fasmora applies site-specific permission controls in addition to Android's app permission controls. Granting an Android permission does not by itself mean every website is permitted to use that resource.

7. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with Fasmora may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the browser
  • Load and display websites
  • Save bookmarks and browser preferences
  • Manage downloads
  • Improve app performance
  • Detect and diagnose crashes or technical problems
  • Maintain security
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal requirements

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product.

8. Data Storage and Security

Some browser information, including bookmarks, cookies, cached website data, browsing-related data, and downloaded files, may be stored locally on your device.

The security of information stored on your device also depends on your device's security settings, operating system, lock screen, and other protections.

While we take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Flux and its supporting services, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Data Sharing

Fasmora may rely on third-party service providers to provide certain functionality, including advertising, analytics, crash reporting, hosting, or other technical services.

Where applicable, these providers may process limited technical information necessary to provide their services.

We may also disclose information when required to:

  • Comply with applicable laws or regulations
  • Respond to valid legal requests
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of Flux, our users, or others
  • Detect or prevent fraud, abuse, or security problems

10. Children's Privacy

Fasmora is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user profiles or selling personal information.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

11. Your Choices

Depending on the features available in your version of Fasmora, you may be able to:

  • Clear browsing history
  • Clear cookies and website data
  • Clear cached data
  • Delete bookmarks
  • Delete downloaded files
  • Disable or manage certain permissions
  • Reset browser data
  • Control advertising preferences through Android or applicable third-party services

You may also uninstall Fasmora at any time.

12. Data Retention

Information stored locally by Fasmora generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the relevant browser data, or uninstall the App.

Information processed by third-party services is retained according to the policies of those services.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by applicable law.

13. International Data Transfers

Third-party service providers used by Fasmora may process information on servers located in countries other than your country of residence.

Where applicable, such providers are responsible for implementing appropriate safeguards for international data transfers in accordance with applicable law.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Fasmora, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

16. Third-Party Services

Fasmora may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies.

Examples may include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, hosting, or other technology providers.

These services may include providers used for advertising, crash diagnostics, notifications, authentication, hosting, and AI processing. Depending on the feature you use, those providers may process technical identifiers, diagnostics, account authentication data, AI request text, or relevant webpage context as described in this Policy.

The specific third-party services used may vary between versions of Fasmora. Their respective privacy policies govern their processing of information.
